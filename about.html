Our Story

Meet Junior and Justin, founders of AKA. We're brothers who grew up together, always chasing a vision of doing something different, something that felt like ours.

We built AKA around one idea: see what others miss. The small details that lift a moment, that shape a story. This was never about the money for us, it's the passion behind it that keeps us moving forward.

It started back in 2023, when we picked up our first camera. Neither of us had a plan to become who we are today. But there was a moment, the moment we both felt it: this is it, this is what we're meant to do. From there, we agreed to build this journey together.

Kanthamet (Junior)

Junior brings a unique spatial perspective to filmmaking. He specializes in uncovering the intricate, overlooked details and elements, connecting them through a visual narrative that blends technical precision with human emotion.

Kanthawat (Justin)

Justin is a visual storyteller who doesn't just capture frames, he builds narratives. Known for a "whatever it takes" mentality, Justin thrives in high-stakes environments, chasing the high-risk, high-reward visuals that others might miss.
