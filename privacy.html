Privacy Policy

Introduction

AKA Motions is a photography and videography studio. This portfolio website exists to showcase our work and make it easy for prospective clients to get in touch. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

We only collect what you choose to give us through the contact form on this site nothing more.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our contact page, we collect:

  • Name - first and last name
  • Email address - to reply to your enquiry
  • Phone number - optional, only if you choose to provide it
  • Message content - whatever details you share about your project

We do not collect any other personal information. This site does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and we do not gather information about you through any means other than the contact form.

How We Use Your Information

The information submitted through the contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Discuss and coordinate potential or ongoing projects with you

We do not use your information for marketing, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your information to any third party.

How Long We Keep It

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and, if a project moves forward, for the duration of that working relationship. If you'd like your information deleted sooner, contact us and we'll remove it.

Third-Party Links

Our site links to third-party platforms, including Instagram, through our official accounts, @aka_motionnn . We do not collect, access, or process any information from Instagram or other third-party social media platforms. We have no control over and are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms. Any information you choose to share with them is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct any information that's inaccurate
  • Delete your information from our records

To make any of these requests, email us at info@akamotions.com.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 16.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to the site or our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, reach out at info@akamotions.com.
